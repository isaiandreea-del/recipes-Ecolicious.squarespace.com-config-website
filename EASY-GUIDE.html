Ecolicious – Easy guide

A. Link TikTok and Instagram (when you have the accounts)

  1. Copy your account address. On TikTok it looks like https://www.tiktok.com/@yourname. On Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/yourname. (Open your profile in a browser and copy the address bar.)
  2. Go to your repository on github.com and click the file index.html.
  3. Click the pencil icon (Edit this file), top right of the file.
  4. Press Ctrl+F (Mac: Cmd+F) and search for TIKTOK_URL.
  5. You will see const TIKTOK_URL = "";. Paste your address between the two quote marks: const TIKTOK_URL = "https://www.tiktok.com/@yourname";
  6. Do the same for INSTAGRAM_URL.
  7. Click the green Commit changes… button, then Commit changes again.
  8. Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh your site. The two icons now appear in the top menu and open your accounts. Until you fill in an address, the icon stays hidden.

B. Change the photo of a recipe

Step 1 – Find the recipe number. Open the file RECIPE-NUMBERS.txt from the zip. It lists every recipe with its number (for example "12 - Pizza Margherita"). Write down the numbers of the recipes you have photos for.

Step 2 – Prepare the photo. Save it as .jpg (or .webp / .png), about 800 pixels wide. Name it with the recipe number, for example 12.jpg.

Step 3 – Upload the photo.

  1. On GitHub, open your repository and click Add file → Upload files.
  2. Drag your photos in (up to 100 at a time).
  3. Click Commit changes.

Step 4 – Tell the app which photo belongs to which recipe.

  1. Click the file photos.json, then the pencil icon.
  2. It contains {}. Replace it with one line per photo, like this (recipe number, then file name): 
    {
  "12": "12.jpg",
  "45": "45.jpg",
  "101": "101.jpg"
}
  3. Rules: every line ends with a comma except the last one; use straight quote marks "; the file name must match exactly, including .jpg.
  4. Click Commit changes.

Step 5 – Check. After 1–2 minutes refresh the site (Ctrl+F5). The new photo shows on the recipe card and at the top of the recipe. If you still see the old picture, refresh again or check the file name.

To go back to the watercolor picture: delete that recipe's line from photos.json and commit.

New features – easy steps

C. Give each Premium customer their own code

  1. Open code-maker.html from the zip by double-clicking it (it opens in your browser).
  2. Type the customer's name, optionally pick "Valid until" (for example one month or one year after they pay), and press Make code.
  3. The code (like ECO-7K2M-9QXA) appears next to the name. Copy it and email it to the customer (for example in your Squarespace order confirmation or a message).
  4. Press Download codes.json.
  5. On GitHub: Add file → Upload files, drag in codes.json, click Commit changes. In 1–2 minutes the code works.
  6. Next time, open code-maker.html and choose your latest codes.json in step 1, so old codes are kept.

The code PREMIUM2026 still works for everyone who types it (use it for yourself and for testing). To switch it off, open index.html on GitHub, find PREMIUM_CODES and delete "PREMIUM2026" from the brackets (leave []).

D. Switch a customer off / let them unsubscribe

  1. Customer cancels or stops paying: open code-maker.html, load your codes.json, press Disable next to their name, download codes.json, and upload it to GitHub (as in C). On their next visit Premium turns off by itself.
  2. Automatic ending: set "Valid until" when you make the code. After that date it stops working.
  3. The customer wants to stop: they click ★ Premium activ at the top, then Turn off Premium on this device. To stop the monthly payment they cancel in their Squarespace account (the same dialog has a "Manage subscription" link to your Login page). Please check in your Squarespace settings how customers cancel a subscription, since this depends on your plan.

E. How the free version works (3 tries a day)

  • Visitors without Premium pick ingredients and press 🔍 Find recipes. Each press that shows new results counts as one try. They get 3 tries per day; the counter resets at midnight. Pressing again with the same ingredients is free.
  • Browsing and searching recipes by name is always free.
  • Premium users have unlimited tries and see results instantly.
  • To change the number, open index.html on GitHub and change const FREE_TRIES = 3;.
  • The count is stored in the visitor's browser, so a technical person can reset it. That is normal for a site without accounts.

F. Add pictures of your products / ingredients (easiest way)

  1. Put your photos in one folder on your computer (any size, .jpg or .png).
  2. Double-click photo-helper.html (from the zip). It opens in your browser. You need internet for it.
  3. Step 1: click "Choose files" and select the photos.
  4. Step 2: for each photo, pick from the list what it shows (an ingredient/product such as "Roșii", or a recipe such as "12 – Pizza Margherita"). The helper guesses from the file name if it can.
  5. Step 3 (optional): if you added photos before, choose your current photos.json and ingredient-photos.json so they are kept.
  6. Step 4: press Make my ZIP. A file ecolicious-photos.zip downloads. Unzip it.
  7. On GitHub: Add file → Upload files. Drag in the photos folder, photos.json and ingredient-photos.json. Click Commit changes.
  8. Wait 1–2 minutes, refresh with Ctrl+F5. Ingredient photos appear as small round pictures on the clipboard chips; recipe photos replace the recipe card picture.

G. Ads (only for visitors without Premium)

  1. Preview: add ?ads=demo to your site address (for example https://yourname.github.io/ecolicious/?ads=demo) to see where the ads go: one small sticky-note ad after the 6th recipe and one slim banner under the recipes. Nothing else changes.
  2. Use the photo-helper (or upload files with Add file → Upload files) to put your ad pictures in the photos folder, for example photos/ad-card.jpg (square-ish) and photos/ad-banner.jpg (wide, about 728×90).
  3. On GitHub open ads.json, click the pencil icon, change "enabled": false to true, and replace the image names, titles and the link addresses with your sponsor's.
  4. Click Commit changes. Premium customers never see ads.
  5. If you use an ad network such as Google AdSense, put its code in the "html" field of banner (ask me and I will prepare it). Ad networks have their own approval rules.
